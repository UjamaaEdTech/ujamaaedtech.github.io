The Design Challenge

Preserve academic requirements while expanding how learning happens.

This curriculum architecture is designed for Grades 7–9 / ages 12–15, combining a Ghana Education Service-oriented subject foundation with inquiry, experiential learning, interdisciplinary connections, and global perspectives.

The design uses an IB-style learner profile and inquiry framework without representing the curriculum as an authorized International Baccalaureate programme.

Subject Foundation → Interdisciplinary Connection → Inquiry → Creation → Assessment → Year 9 Capstone
Core Design Framework

Inquiry, learner development, and connected disciplines.

Learner Profile

Inquirers, knowledgeable, thinkers, communicators, principled, open-minded, caring, risk-takers, balanced, and reflective.

Inquiry + Experience

Learning is organized around investigation, application, creation, communication, and reflection.

Multiple Evidence Sources

Assessment can combine examinations with portfolios, reflections, presentations, service projects, peer/self-assessment, and inquiry exhibitions.
