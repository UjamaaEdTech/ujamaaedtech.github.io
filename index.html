Women's Leadership & Life Coaching: A framework for building what comes next.

E.M.P.O.W.E.R. Her™ is a seven-stage life-coaching and women's leadership framework for women building their next chapter through entrepreneurship, professional growth, leadership, and personal development.

Life CoachingStructured coaching for personal and professional development
EntrepreneurshipBuild, clarify, and strengthen the next chapter
Women's LeadershipLeadership development for women in tech, STEM, and beyond
Signature FrameworkSeven stages with each stage as a complete coaching lesson

Seven stages. One integrated coaching journey.

The E.M.P.O.W.E.R. Her™ Framework creates a structured coaching experience that can support individual coaching, cohort experiences, and future program offerings. The framework is informed by NLP certification training.
